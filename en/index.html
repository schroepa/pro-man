Less tool chaos. More clarity.

Solo, you need clarity without admin overhead. As a studio, you need a shared place — that stays with you, not in someone else’s cloud.

  • Instead of five tabs

    Kanban, list, Gantt, calendar, docs, and knowledge in one workspace — no hopping between tools.

  • Instead of another SaaS seat

    No account, no seats, no telemetry. Files stay folders on disk or the NAS.

  • Instead of scattered notes

    Markdown that Obsidian and Git understand — ProMan is the surface, the vault stays yours.

Why not another SaaS?

Notion, Trello, and Jira solve work — and lock data, accounts, and workflows to a vendor.

Accounts & seats
SaaS: login, licenses, onboardingProMan: open and work — no account
Data ownership
SaaS: data in their cloudProMan: Markdown files on your side
Offline & folders
SaaS: browser + sync requiredProMan: vault folder locally or on a share
Small team
SaaS: sync server & workspace adminProMan: same folder, “I am …”, reload
